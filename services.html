FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does a typical website project take?
Most marketing websites launch in 2–4 weeks. E-commerce and larger platforms typically take 4–8 weeks, depending on scope. We share a clear timeline after discovery and keep you updated in Slack/Notion throughout.
Which platform will my website be built on?
We recommend the platform that fits your goals — Framer or Webflow for speed and stunning interactions, WordPress for content-heavy sites, Shopify or WooCommerce for stores. You'll always own your site and content.
Do you handle SEO and marketing after launch?
Yes. Every build ships with on-page SEO baked in, and our Growth Plan includes ongoing SEO, analytics reporting and content updates. We also run email and SEM campaigns as add-ons.
Can I update the website myself?
Absolutely. We integrate a CMS so you can edit text, images and posts without code — and we'll train you on it. Prefer hands-off? Our plans include managed content updates.
How do payments work?
Subscription plans are billed monthly via Flutterwave. Custom projects are typically split into milestone payments. No hidden fees, cancel any time with notice.
