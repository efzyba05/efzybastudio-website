Our Story

Hi, I'm Emmanuel — founder of Efzyba Studio

I'm a passionate web designer inspired by the ever-evolving world of design, where creativity meets purpose. I thrive on transforming bold ideas into visually stunning, functional interfaces that not only look great but make a meaningful impact.

With an MBA in International Digital Marketing and Business Transformation from Rome Business School, I blend design with strategy to create solutions that resonate with users and achieve real business goals. Efzyba Studio grew from that mission — a senior, multidisciplinary team delivering websites, data and growth under one roof.

  • No-code mastery: Framer, Webflow, WordPress, Wix & Shopify
  • Interface design in Figma, grounded in user research
  • Growth stack: SEO, SEM, email marketing & social strategy
  • Data-driven decisions with analytics & reporting baked in
