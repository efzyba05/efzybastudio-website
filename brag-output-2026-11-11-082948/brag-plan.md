# Efzyba Studio — vertical social cut (9:16)

The same story as the landscape film, recomposed for a phone screen (Reels, TikTok, Stories). 1080×1920, 30fps, 30 beats at 96 BPM = 18.75s. Polished tone.

**Hook:** the gold-foil Ef-S monogram stamped onto black, with the light sweeping across it.
**Change from the landscape cut:** the "page" scene shows the real luxe page at phone width inside a phone, because that's where this audience watches.

## Music
96 BPM (1 beat = 0.625s), D minor: Dm9 → Bbmaj7 → Gm9 → A7sus4. Same palette as the landscape score, re-cut to this grid. The stamp boom lands on beat 1 and the click and bell on beat 28.
