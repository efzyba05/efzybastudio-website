## Platform

web

## Users
Primary (confirmed for the luxury landing page): founders and marketing leads at premium, ambitious brands who want a senior studio to design, build and grow their digital presence. Their job on the page: decide whether Efzyba is the calibre of partner they want, then book a free 30-minute strategy call.
Secondary (existing site): SMEs and startups in Nigeria, the UK and worldwide.

## Product Purpose
Efzyba Studio is a full-stack creative agency: websites, e-commerce, landing pages, UI/UX & prototyping, brand identity, SEO & digital marketing, data analytics, and UK-based virtual assistance. Success: qualified strategy calls booked.

## Positioning
A compact, senior, multidisciplinary team (design, data, marketing, operations) under one roof, spanning Lagos and London. Founder Emmanuel Akinola blends design with MBA-grade marketing strategy (MBA, International Digital Marketing & Business Transformation, Rome Business School). Data analytics is in-house, so builds are measured, not just launched.

## Operating Context
Discover → Design → Build → Grow. Built in Framer, Webflow, WordPress, Shopify/WooCommerce; designed in Figma; run in Notion/Slack. Marketing sites launch in 2–4 weeks, e-commerce/platforms in 4–8 weeks. Replies within one business day.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS site deployed on Netlify (push to main auto-deploys).
- Luxury landing page lives as a standalone page (`luxe/index.html`); it does not replace the homepage unless the owner decides later.
- Pricing ($750 / $1,200 / Custom monthly plans) is deliberately NOT shown on the luxury page.
