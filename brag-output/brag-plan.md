# Efzyba Studio — "The Annual Review" launch film

**What:** Efzyba Studio is a senior design, data and growth studio in Lagos and London. Its new landing page presents the studio's work like a private bank's annual report.
**For:** premium brands choosing a web/digital partner. **Action:** book a private strategy call.
**Sets it apart:** results measured after launch (+85% qualified leads, +72% bookings, 2.3x demo requests), all from the studio's own case studies.
**Hook:** the gold-foil Ef-S monogram stamped onto black board, with the light sweeping across it.
**Tone:** polished: restrained, long holds, dips through black.
**Identity:** ink #0d0b08, paper #ece3cf, foil #c9a65a / #ead6a0; Bodoni Moda (display) and Hanken Grotesk (text), the page's own self-hosted fonts.
**Share line:** Six years, 120+ websites, and every launch measured. Efzyba Studio, presented as an annual review.

## Music
96 BPM (1 beat = 0.625s), D minor: Dm9 → Bbmaj7 → Gm9 → A7sus4, felt-piano chords, sub bass, soft kick and brushed shaker. A low boom and a bell on D mark the stamp; the effects stay on the same grid. 34 beats = 21.25s. Every cut falls on a beat.
